# Presensia Brand System

## Presence Portal

The Presensia mark combines four product truths in one reduced symbol:

- the outer arch is an entry portal;
- its silhouette forms an abstract `P`;
- the olive point represents the participant being present;
- the angled exit completes a checkmark for verified attendance.

The mark supports the product promise: **Hadir. Tercatat. Terpantau.**

## Usage

- Use `brand/presensia-mark.svg` on cream or white surfaces.
- Use `brand/presensia-mark-reverse.svg` on dark surfaces.
- Use `brand/presensia-mark-mono.svg` where only one color is available.
- Keep clear space equal to the olive point diameter around the mark.
- Do not redraw, rotate, outline, recolor, or place the primary mark inside an extra rounded square.
- App icons use `brand/presensia-app-icon.svg`; their platform mask supplies the outer shape.

## Minimum Size

- Digital symbol: 16 px minimum.
- Symbol with wordmark: 32 px symbol minimum.
- At 16 px, preserve the olive point but do not add any additional detail.
